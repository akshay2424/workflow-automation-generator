SOP: IT Incident Response
Department: Site Reliability Engineering

1. An alert fires from the monitoring system, or an employee reports an
   issue through the helpdesk.
2. The on-call engineer acknowledges the alert and checks the severity
   level.
3. If the severity is Low, the engineer logs the issue as a ticket for
   the next business day and no further action is taken tonight.
4. If the severity is Medium or High, the engineer begins investigating
   immediately and posts a status update in the incident channel.
5. The engineer checks whether the issue can be resolved by restarting
   the affected service.
   - If a restart resolves it, the engineer confirms the service is
     healthy, updates the incident channel, and closes the incident.
   - If a restart does not resolve it, the engineer escalates to the
     service owner team.
6. The service owner team investigates the root cause.
7. If the root cause requires a code fix, the team opens an emergency
   deployment request, which must be approved by the Engineering Manager
   before it can be deployed.
8. If the Engineering Manager rejects the emergency deployment, the team
   applies a temporary mitigation instead (e.g. feature flag disable,
   traffic rerouting) and schedules the real fix for the next sprint.
9. Once the fix or mitigation is deployed and verified, the incident is
   marked resolved.
10. Within 48 hours of resolution, the on-call engineer writes a
    post-incident report summarizing the timeline, root cause, and
    follow-up actions, and shares it with the wider engineering team.
