Policy: Employee Expense Approval
Effective for all expense claims submitted through the company expense system.

1. Employee submits an expense claim with receipts attached.
2. The system automatically checks whether receipts are attached for all
   line items over $25.
3. If receipts are missing for required line items, the claim is returned
   to the employee for correction.
4. If receipts are complete, the claim proceeds to the employee's direct
   manager for review.
5. The manager checks the total claim amount.
   - If the total is under $1,000, the manager can approve it directly.
   - If the total is $1,000 or more, the claim must also be approved by the
     Department Head.
6. If the Department Head rejects the claim, the employee is notified with
   the rejection reason and the claim is closed.
7. If approved (by manager alone, or manager plus Department Head), the
   claim moves to Payroll for reimbursement processing.
8. Payroll processes the reimbursement in the next pay cycle and the
   employee is notified that reimbursement is complete.
