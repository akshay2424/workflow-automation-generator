Ticket #4821 - New Employee IT Onboarding Request
Requested by: hr-team@company.com
Employee: EMP-10234, contact: new.hire@company.com, phone 555-201-9988

Description:
HR has submitted a new hire request. IT needs to complete the following
before the employee's start date:

1. HR submits the onboarding ticket with employee details and start date.
2. IT Helpdesk reviews the ticket and checks if a laptop is available in
   inventory.
3. If a laptop is available, IT assigns it and proceeds to account
   provisioning.
4. If no laptop is available, IT places a procurement order and the ticket
   is put on hold until the laptop arrives, then proceeds to account
   provisioning.
5. IT provisions email and SSO accounts for the employee.
6. IT Security reviews the account provisioning request and grants access
   based on the employee's department and role.
7. If IT Security flags the request (e.g. role requires elevated access),
   it is escalated to the Security Lead for manual approval.
8. Once access is granted, IT ships or hands off the laptop to the employee
   and marks the ticket as ready for the employee's first day.
9. On the employee's first day, IT confirms login success and closes the
   ticket.
